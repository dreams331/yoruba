As part of the ongoing Presidential CNG Initiative (PCNGI), President Tinubu has directed the rollout of 500 more CNG stations nationwide, doubling the original target to 1,000. The expansion is aimed at giving commercial and private transporters — including in Lagos, Oyo, Ogun, Osun, Ondo, and Ekiti — a cheaper, cleaner alternative to petrol, helping to bring down the cost of transportation for ordinary citizens.

This builds on the administration's broader energy reform agenda following the removal of the petrol subsidy, with the CNG program designed to cushion the transition and improve affordability across Yoruba-populated states and beyond.
