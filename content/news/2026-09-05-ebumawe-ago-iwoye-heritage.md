The traditional ruler of Ago-Iwoye in Ogun State, Oba Rasak Adenugba (the Ebumawe), has highlighted the urgent need for greater youth involvement and institutional backing to safeguard the town's rich Yoruba cultural heritage.

Traditional rulers such as the Ebumawe are custodians of festivals, language and history in their towns. Concerns about young people leaving for the cities or abroad, and about fading knowledge of local customs, are common across Yorubaland, which is why palaces increasingly partner with schools and cultural groups.
