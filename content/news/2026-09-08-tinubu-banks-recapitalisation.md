Speaking on the outcomes of Nigeria's bank recapitalisation drive, President Bola Tinubu called on financial institutions to channel the freshly raised capital into productive investment — manufacturing, agriculture, and job-creating enterprises — rather than short-term financial plays.

The recapitalisation program, part of the administration's broader economic reform agenda, is intended to strengthen the banking sector's capacity to fund large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects across the country, including in the Southwest's manufacturing and agribusiness hubs.
