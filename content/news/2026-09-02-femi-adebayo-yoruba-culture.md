In a candid interview, popular Yoruba actor and producer Femi Adebayo — known for films like "Jagun Jagun" and "King of Thieves" — spoke about the enduring importance of prostrating before an Oba (traditional ruler) as an act of cultural respect, regardless of one's religious background.

Prostration is a long-standing Yoruba gesture of respect, with men lying flat on the ground (ìdọ̀bálẹ̀) and women kneeling (ìkúnlẹ̀) when greeting elders and traditional rulers. Because the gesture expresses courtesy rather than worship, many Yoruba Muslims and Christians keep it alongside their faith, which is the point the actor makes.
