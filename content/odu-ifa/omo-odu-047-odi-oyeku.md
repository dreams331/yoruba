## Òdí-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Overview

A hidden process meets the ancestral realm. The figure points to inherited patterns, transformation and the quiet work of renewal.

### Governing Influences

- **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)


### Guidance

Honour your lineage and allow necessary changes to unfold slowly.

---

*Òdí-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
