## Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òfún

### Overview

Restlessness settles into integrity. Stability is found through honest living, not through control of events.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)


### Guidance

Build peace on honest conduct and let the rest follow.

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (double · double · single · single) and the left column is Òfún Méjì (double · single · double · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òfún means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì

Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. It reminds us that fortunes change, so one must remain adaptable, avoid arrogance, and treat others well on the way up.

*Ohun tó dúró jẹ́ẹ́, ni yíò yí padà.*  
"What stands firm today may be overturned tomorrow."

### From Òfún Méjì

Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact.

*Funfun ni àrà; kò gba ìdọ̀tí láti dúró funfun.*  
"White is sacred; it takes discipline to remain unstained."

### Putting the two together

This figure suggests that a position held today can change tomorrow, so kindness and good relationships are better security than status. It also advises preparing for uncertainty rather than pretending it will not come. At the same time, in daily terms, this figure advises keeping one's word, being slow to anger, and finishing what one starts. It is also a reminder that integrity is maintained through small choices made consistently.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
