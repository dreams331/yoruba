## Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òfún

### Meaning

Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òfún combines the energy of Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility) with Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òfún appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility — must be balanced with the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity. Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì advises: Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. Òfún Méjì adds: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òfún is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
