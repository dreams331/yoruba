## Ọ̀sá-Òtúrúpọ̀n

### Overview

Stress shows in the body. Turmoil affects health and that rest and treatment come first.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Seek care and rest while life is unsettled.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ọ̀sá Méjì (double · single · single · single) and the left column is Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (double · double · single · double). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ọ̀sá-Òtúrúpọ̀n means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ọ̀sá Méjì

Ọ̀sá teaches resilience through sudden disruption. What feels destructive can also clear away what no longer serves growth. It calls for protection (ẹbọ) and steady faith during turbulent seasons.

*Afẹ́fẹ́ tó le kì í fi ìgi sílẹ̀ láìṣí àyípadà.*  
"A strong wind never leaves a tree unchanged."

### From Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì

Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. It also symbolises the wisdom that comes from surviving hardship.

*Àrùn tí a kò tọ́jú lọ́nà tí ó tọ́ kì í yá.*  
"An illness not treated in the right way does not heal."

### Putting the two together

Practically, this figure advises preparation and calm. Keep essentials secure, avoid reacting to every disturbance, and look for what the change makes possible once it settles. At the same time, read in daily life, this figure advises seeking the right help early, following treatment through, and resting enough. It also encourages care for people who are unwell, since recovery is helped by support.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
