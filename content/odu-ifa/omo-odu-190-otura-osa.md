## Òtúrá-Ọ̀sá

### Overview

Calm after the storm. The dust settles and the situation can be read again.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)
- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)


### Guidance

Wait for the turbulence to pass before you decide.

---

*Òtúrá-Ọ̀sá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
