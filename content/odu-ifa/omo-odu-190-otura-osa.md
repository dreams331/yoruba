## Òtúrá-Ọ̀sá

### Meaning

Òtúrá-Ọ̀sá combines the energy of Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed) with Ọ̀sá Méjì (upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)
- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)

### Guidance

When Òtúrá-Ọ̀sá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀sá Méjì — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm. Òtúrá Méjì advises: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Ọ̀sá Méjì adds: Ọ̀sá teaches resilience through sudden disruption. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òtúrá-Ọ̀sá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
