## Òtúrá-ÈjìOgbè

### Overview

Clarity arrives with the dawn. A situation that has felt muddy begins to make sense, and a new beginning follows.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)
- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)


### Guidance

Make your move once the picture is clear.

---

*Òtúrá-ÈjìOgbè is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
