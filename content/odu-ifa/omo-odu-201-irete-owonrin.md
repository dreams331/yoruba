## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀wọ́nrín

### Overview

Staying steady through change. A world in motion tests your resolve, and those who adjust come out ahead.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Keep your goal fixed and your methods flexible.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (single · single · double · single) and the left column is Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (double · double · single · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀wọ́nrín means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì

Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. It reassures that persistence — even quiet, unglamorous effort — leads to eventual triumph.

*Ìṣẹ́gun tí a kò fojú rí ni í dùn jù.*  
"The victory unseen by others is often the sweetest."

### From Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì

Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. It reminds us that fortunes change, so one must remain adaptable, avoid arrogance, and treat others well on the way up.

*Ohun tó dúró jẹ́ẹ́, ni yíò yí padà.*  
"What stands firm today may be overturned tomorrow."

### Putting the two together

This figure reads as reassurance for those who feel stuck. Keep going, keep records of progress, and avoid measuring yourself against people whose circumstances differ. Results often arrive later than hoped but are lasting when earned. At the same time, this figure suggests that a position held today can change tomorrow, so kindness and good relationships are better security than status. It also advises preparing for uncertainty rather than pretending it will not come.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
