## Ọ̀kànràn-Ìrosùn

### Overview

Conflict has consequences. The figure warns that anger can lead to injury or loss, and that sacrifice and restraint bring peace.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)


### Guidance

Cool tempers, make amends and pay what is owed.

*Ọ̀kànràn-Ìrosùn is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
