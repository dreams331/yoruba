## Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀bàrà

### Overview

Pride is blown off its pedestal. Take care here: the whirlwind humbles the overconfident and spares the humble.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)


### Guidance

Stay modest, and prepare for a change in position.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ọ̀sá Méjì (double · single · single · single) and the left column is Ọ̀bàrà Méjì (single · double · double · double). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀bàrà means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ọ̀sá Méjì

Ọ̀sá teaches resilience through sudden disruption. What feels destructive can also clear away what no longer serves growth. It calls for protection (ẹbọ) and steady faith during turbulent seasons.

*Afẹ́fẹ́ tó le kì í fi ìgi sílẹ̀ láìṣí àyípadà.*  
"A strong wind never leaves a tree unchanged."

### From Ọ̀bàrà Méjì

Ọ̀bàrà teaches that leadership and confidence are gifts, but boastfulness invites downfall. True honour is shown through humility and generosity, not empty declarations.

*Ìgbéraga ni í tẹ̀lé àṣejù ìṣọ́ra.*  
"Excess confidence is often followed by a fall."

### Putting the two together

Practically, this figure advises preparation and calm. Keep essentials secure, avoid reacting to every disturbance, and look for what the change makes possible once it settles. At the same time, read practically, this figure advises that a person should let results speak. Say less than you can do, deliver what you say, and share credit. Reputation built that way is stronger than one built on announcements.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
