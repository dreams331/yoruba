## Òfún-Ọ̀bàrà

### Meaning

Òfún-Ọ̀bàrà combines the energy of Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity) with Ọ̀bàrà Méjì (honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)

### Guidance

When Òfún-Ọ̀bàrà appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀bàrà Méjì — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership. Òfún Méjì advises: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Ọ̀bàrà Méjì adds: Ọ̀bàrà teaches that leadership and confidence are gifts, but boastfulness invites downfall. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*Òfún-Ọ̀bàrà is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
