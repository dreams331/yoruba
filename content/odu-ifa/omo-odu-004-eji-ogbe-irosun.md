## Ogbè-Ìrosùn

### Overview

Divine favour is tied to sacrifice. Blessings are available, but they follow a willingness to give something up and to keep promises made to the Òrìṣà.

### Governing Influences

* **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)
* **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)


### Guidance

Make the offering or commitment you have been postponing, and keep your word.
