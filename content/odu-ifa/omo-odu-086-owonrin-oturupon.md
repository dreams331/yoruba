## Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òtúrúpọ̀n

### Overview

Changes affect the body and health. It advises rest, regular care and patience during recovery.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Look after your wellbeing while circumstances shift.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (double · double · single · single) and the left column is Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (double · double · single · double). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Òtúrúpọ̀n means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì

Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. It reminds us that fortunes change, so one must remain adaptable, avoid arrogance, and treat others well on the way up.

*Ohun tó dúró jẹ́ẹ́, ni yíò yí padà.*  
"What stands firm today may be overturned tomorrow."

### From Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì

Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. It also symbolises the wisdom that comes from surviving hardship.

*Àrùn tí a kò tọ́jú lọ́nà tí ó tọ́ kì í yá.*  
"An illness not treated in the right way does not heal."

### Putting the two together

This figure suggests that a position held today can change tomorrow, so kindness and good relationships are better security than status. It also advises preparing for uncertainty rather than pretending it will not come. At the same time, read in daily life, this figure advises seeking the right help early, following treatment through, and resting enough. It also encourages care for people who are unwell, since recovery is helped by support.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
