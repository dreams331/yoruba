## Ọ̀kànràn-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Meaning

Ọ̀kànràn-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú combines the energy of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences) with Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀kànràn-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality. Ọ̀kànràn Méjì advises: Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì adds: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀kànràn-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
