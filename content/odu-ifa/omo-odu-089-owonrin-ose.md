## Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Ọ̀ṣẹ́

### Meaning

Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ combines the energy of Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility) with Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude. Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì advises: Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì adds: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
