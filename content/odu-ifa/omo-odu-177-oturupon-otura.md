## Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òtúrá

### Meaning

Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òtúrá combines the energy of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience) with Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)

### Guidance

When Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òtúrá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience — must be balanced with the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed. Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì advises: Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. Òtúrá Méjì adds: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òtúrá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
