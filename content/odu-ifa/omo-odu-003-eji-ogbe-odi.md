## Ogbè-Òdí

### Overview

Open possibility enters a closed, mysterious space. Something promising is gestating and cannot be hurried; the light is present even where it is not yet visible.

### Governing Influences

* **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)
* **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)


### Guidance

Be patient with projects still taking shape and protect them from premature exposure.

*Ogbè-Òdí is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
