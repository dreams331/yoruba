## Ogbè-Ògúndá

### Overview

Opportunity arrives with hard work attached. The way forward is open, but it must be cleared by effort, skill and perseverance.

### Governing Influences

* **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)
* **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)


### Guidance

Take up your tools and work steadily; the path will respond to determination.

*Ogbè-Ògúndá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
