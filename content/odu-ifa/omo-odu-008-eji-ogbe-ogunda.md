## ÈjìOgbè-Ògúndá

### Meaning

ÈjìOgbè-Ògúndá combines the energy of Èjì Ogbè (light, new beginnings, divine potential) with Ògúndá Méjì (struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)
- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)

### Guidance

When ÈjìOgbè-Ògúndá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Èjì Ogbè — light, new beginnings, divine potential — must be balanced with the lessons of Ògúndá Méjì — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path. Èjì Ogbè advises: You were not born by accident. Ògúndá Méjì adds: Ògúndá honours the warrior spirit of Ògún — perseverance, the courage to clear obstacles, and the discipline of using one's tools and skills wisely rather than recklessly. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*ÈjìOgbè-Ògúndá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
