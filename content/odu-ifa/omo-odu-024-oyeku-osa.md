## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀sá

### Meaning

Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀sá combines the energy of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality) with Ọ̀sá Méjì (upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀sá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀sá Méjì — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì advises: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Ọ̀sá Méjì adds: Ọ̀sá teaches resilience through sudden disruption. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀sá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
