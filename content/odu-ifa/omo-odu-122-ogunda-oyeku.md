## Ògúndá-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Overview

Labour is tied to the ancestors. The figure says that skills and trades are inherited, and that honouring the source of your craft protects its future.

### Governing Influences

- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)


### Guidance

Respect the people who taught you and pass your skill on.

*Ògúndá-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
