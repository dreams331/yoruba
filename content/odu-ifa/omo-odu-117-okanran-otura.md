## Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrá

### Meaning

Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrá combines the energy of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences) with Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences — must be balanced with the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed. Ọ̀kànràn Méjì advises: Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. Òtúrá Méjì adds: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
