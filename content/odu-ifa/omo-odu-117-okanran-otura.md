## Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrá

### Overview

Truth clears the air. After a period of confusion and argument, the facts come out and a settlement becomes possible.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)


### Guidance

Let the facts speak, and settle the matter once they are clear.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (double · double · double · single) and the left column is Òtúrá Méjì (single · double · single · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrá means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ọ̀kànràn Méjì

Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. It teaches that confronting conflict honestly, though painful, prevents greater harm later.

*Òtítọ́ máa ń dùn, ṣùgbọ́n irọ́ máa ń pa ni níkẹyìn.*  
"Truth may sting, but a lie eventually destroys."

### From Òtúrá Méjì

Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. It marks turning points and the wisdom gained through transformation.

*Kùràkùrà ọjọ́ ni ìmọ́lẹ̀ ọjọ́ tuntun.*  
"The dimness before dawn gives way to the light of a new day."

### Putting the two together

In everyday terms, this figure advises resolving disputes directly rather than letting them grow in silence. It also asks a person to think before speaking, because words once said cannot be taken back. At the same time, in practice, the figure encourages a person not to make final decisions in the middle of confusion. Wait for understanding, speak calmly, and expect that the picture will clear for those who stay honest and patient.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
