## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìrosùn

### Overview

Loss and consequence are close together. The figure warns of the cost of careless actions and of the need for offerings to protect the living.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)


### Guidance

Make amends where needed and perform the sacrifice that restores balance.

---

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìrosùn is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
