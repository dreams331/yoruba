## Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òtúrá

### Meaning

Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òtúrá combines the energy of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude) with Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òtúrá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude — must be balanced with the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì advises: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Òtúrá Méjì adds: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òtúrá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
