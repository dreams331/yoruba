## Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀wọ́nrín

### Overview

Double instability. The figure warns that everything is moving at once and advises caution with commitments until things settle.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Delay major decisions until the situation stabilises.

---

*Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀wọ́nrín is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
