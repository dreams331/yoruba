## Ògúndá-ÈjìOgbè

### Overview

Effort walks under a bright sky. The figure promises that hard work done at the right time is met with favourable conditions.

### Governing Influences

- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)
- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)


### Guidance

Start the work you have been postponing; conditions favour you.

---

*Ògúndá-ÈjìOgbè is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
