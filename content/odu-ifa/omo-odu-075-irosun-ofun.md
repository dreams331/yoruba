## Ìrosùn-Òfún

### Meaning

Ìrosùn-Òfún combines the energy of Ìrosùn Méjì (blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence) with Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)
- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Ìrosùn-Òfún appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìrosùn Méjì — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence — must be balanced with the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity. Ìrosùn Méjì advises: Ìrosùn warns that actions have irreversible consequences, and that ẹbọ (sacrifice) — whether of time, pride, or resources — is often required to avert disaster. Òfún Méjì adds: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìrosùn-Òfún is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
