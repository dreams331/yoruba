## Ìwòrì-Ọ̀ṣẹ́

### Overview

Wisdom brings sweetness. Knowing yourself leads to contentment, and the figure encourages gratitude for what you already have.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)


### Guidance

Appreciate your blessings, and share the joy that comes from a clear conscience.
