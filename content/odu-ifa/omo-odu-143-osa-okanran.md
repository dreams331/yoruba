## Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀kànràn

### Overview

Upheaval brings open quarrel. The figure advises restraint of speech while emotions are high.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)


### Guidance

Hold your words until tempers have cooled.

*Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀kànràn is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
