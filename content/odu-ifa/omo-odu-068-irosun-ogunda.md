## Ìrosùn-Ògúndá

### Overview

Hard work and offerings go hand in hand. The path ahead is clearable, but only if effort is paired with respect for spiritual duty.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)
- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)


### Guidance

Work steadily, and honour your commitments to the Òrìṣà.

*Ìrosùn-Ògúndá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
