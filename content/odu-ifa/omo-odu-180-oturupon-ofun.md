## Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òfún

### Meaning

Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òfún combines the energy of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience) with Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òfún appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience — must be balanced with the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity. Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì advises: Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. Òfún Méjì adds: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òtúrúpọ̀n-Òfún is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
