## Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Overview

Turbulence stirs the ancestors. Upheaval is a time to seek the guidance of elders and the departed.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)


### Guidance

Consult the wise and remember your roots when things shake.

*Ọ̀sá-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
