## Ọ̀bàrà-ÈjìOgbè

### Meaning

Ọ̀bàrà-ÈjìOgbè combines the energy of Ọ̀bàrà Méjì (honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership) with Èjì Ogbè (light, new beginnings, divine potential). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)
- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀bàrà-ÈjìOgbè appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀bàrà Méjì — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership — must be balanced with the lessons of Èjì Ogbè — light, new beginnings, divine potential. Ọ̀bàrà Méjì advises: Ọ̀bàrà teaches that leadership and confidence are gifts, but boastfulness invites downfall. Èjì Ogbè adds: You were not born by accident. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*Ọ̀bàrà-ÈjìOgbè is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
