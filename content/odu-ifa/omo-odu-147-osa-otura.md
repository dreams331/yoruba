## Ọ̀sá-Òtúrá

### Overview

The storm passes and the view clears. Things will make sense once the disruption subsides.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)


### Guidance

Be patient; the aftermath will be easier to read.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ọ̀sá Méjì (double · single · single · single) and the left column is Òtúrá Méjì (single · double · single · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ọ̀sá-Òtúrá means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ọ̀sá Méjì

Ọ̀sá teaches resilience through sudden disruption. What feels destructive can also clear away what no longer serves growth. It calls for protection (ẹbọ) and steady faith during turbulent seasons.

*Afẹ́fẹ́ tó le kì í fi ìgi sílẹ̀ láìṣí àyípadà.*  
"A strong wind never leaves a tree unchanged."

### From Òtúrá Méjì

Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. It marks turning points and the wisdom gained through transformation.

*Kùràkùrà ọjọ́ ni ìmọ́lẹ̀ ọjọ́ tuntun.*  
"The dimness before dawn gives way to the light of a new day."

### Putting the two together

Practically, this figure advises preparation and calm. Keep essentials secure, avoid reacting to every disturbance, and look for what the change makes possible once it settles. At the same time, in practice, the figure encourages a person not to make final decisions in the middle of confusion. Wait for understanding, speak calmly, and expect that the picture will clear for those who stay honest and patient.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
