## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òtúrá

### Meaning

Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òtúrá combines the energy of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory) with Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)

### Guidance

When Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òtúrá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory — must be balanced with the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed. Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì advises: Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. Òtúrá Méjì adds: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òtúrá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
