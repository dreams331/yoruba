## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀ṣẹ́

### Meaning

Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ combines the energy of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory) with Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)

### Guidance

When Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude. Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì advises: Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì adds: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
