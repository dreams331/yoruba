## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀ṣẹ́

### Overview

A reward at the end of a long road. Those who persevere receive something sweet, and it is meant to be shared.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)


### Guidance

Celebrate the finish and share it with those who helped.

---

*Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
