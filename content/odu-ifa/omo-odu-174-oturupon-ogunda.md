## Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ògúndá

### Meaning

Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ògúndá combines the energy of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience) with Ògúndá Méjì (struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)

### Guidance

When Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ògúndá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience — must be balanced with the lessons of Ògúndá Méjì — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path. Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì advises: Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. Ògúndá Méjì adds: Ògúndá honours the warrior spirit of Ògún — perseverance, the courage to clear obstacles, and the discipline of using one's tools and skills wisely rather than recklessly. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ògúndá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
