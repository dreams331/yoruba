## Òfún-ÈjìOgbè

### Meaning

Òfún-ÈjìOgbè combines the energy of Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity) with Èjì Ogbè (light, new beginnings, divine potential). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)
- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Òfún-ÈjìOgbè appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity — must be balanced with the lessons of Èjì Ogbè — light, new beginnings, divine potential. Òfún Méjì advises: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Èjì Ogbè adds: You were not born by accident. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òfún-ÈjìOgbè is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
