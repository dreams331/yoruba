## Ìká-Òdí

### Overview

Hidden ill will. There is a warning of resentment that is concealed, and advises watchfulness and quiet protective action.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)
- **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)


### Guidance

Stay discreet and observant around those whose motives are unclear.

---

*Ìká-Òdí is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
