## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìwòrì

### Meaning

Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìwòrì combines the energy of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality) with Ìwòrì Méjì (self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìwòrì appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality — must be balanced with the lessons of Ìwòrì Méjì — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì advises: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Ìwòrì Méjì adds: Ìwòrì calls for introspection and honesty about one's own motives before judging others. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìwòrì is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
