## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìwòrì

### Overview

Ancestral memory meets inner sight. This figure says that the answers you seek come through honest self-examination and respect for the departed.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Remember where you come from, and let that knowledge shape who you decide to be.

---

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ìwòrì is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
