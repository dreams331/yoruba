## Ìrosùn-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Overview

Sacrifice meets mortality. The figure warns that careless action can bring loss, and urges care for the family and the elderly.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)


### Guidance

Act prudently, protect the vulnerable, and make the offering that restores balance.

*Ìrosùn-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
