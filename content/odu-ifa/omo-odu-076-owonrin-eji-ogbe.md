## Ọ̀wọ́nrín-ÈjìOgbè

### Meaning

Ọ̀wọ́nrín-ÈjìOgbè combines the energy of Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility) with Èjì Ogbè (light, new beginnings, divine potential). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀wọ́nrín-ÈjìOgbè appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility — must be balanced with the lessons of Èjì Ogbè — light, new beginnings, divine potential. Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì advises: Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. Èjì Ogbè adds: You were not born by accident. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

*Ọ̀wọ́nrín-ÈjìOgbè is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
