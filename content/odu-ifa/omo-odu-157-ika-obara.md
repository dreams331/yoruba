## Ìká-Ọ̀bàrà

### Meaning

Ìká-Ọ̀bàrà combines the energy of Ìká Méjì (cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint) with Ọ̀bàrà Méjì (honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)

### Guidance

When Ìká-Ọ̀bàrà appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìká Méjì — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀bàrà Méjì — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership. Ìká Méjì advises: Ìká warns of the dangers of bitterness, envy, and vengeance — both suffering it and inflicting it. Ọ̀bàrà Méjì adds: Ọ̀bàrà teaches that leadership and confidence are gifts, but boastfulness invites downfall. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìká-Ọ̀bàrà is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
