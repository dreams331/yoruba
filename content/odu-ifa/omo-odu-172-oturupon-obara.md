## Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ọ̀bàrà

### Overview

Recovery tests the ego. A proud person finds it hard to accept help, and healing waits until they do.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)


### Guidance

Accept care from others without embarrassment.

*Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ọ̀bàrà is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
