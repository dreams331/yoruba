## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ìká

### Meaning

Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ìká combines the energy of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory) with Ìká Méjì (cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)

### Guidance

When Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ìká appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory — must be balanced with the lessons of Ìká Méjì — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint. Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì advises: Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. Ìká Méjì adds: Ìká warns of the dangers of bitterness, envy, and vengeance — both suffering it and inflicting it. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ìká is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
