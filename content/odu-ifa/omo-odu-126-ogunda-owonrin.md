## Ògúndá-Ọ̀wọ́nrín

### Overview

Effort meets changing conditions. The worker who adjusts methods without losing purpose will prevail.

### Governing Influences

- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)
- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Change your approach as needed while keeping your aim.

*Ògúndá-Ọ̀wọ́nrín is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
