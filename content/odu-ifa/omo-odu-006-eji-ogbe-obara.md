## ÈjìOgbè-Ọ̀bàrà

### Meaning

ÈjìOgbè-Ọ̀bàrà combines the energy of Èjì Ogbè (light, new beginnings, divine potential) with Ọ̀bàrà Méjì (honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)

### Guidance

When ÈjìOgbè-Ọ̀bàrà appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Èjì Ogbè — light, new beginnings, divine potential — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀bàrà Méjì — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership. Èjì Ogbè advises: You were not born by accident. Ọ̀bàrà Méjì adds: Ọ̀bàrà teaches that leadership and confidence are gifts, but boastfulness invites downfall. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*ÈjìOgbè-Ọ̀bàrà is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
