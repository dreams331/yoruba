## Ògúndá-Ọ̀kànràn

### Overview

Struggle meets a sharp dispute. There is a warning of friction among colleagues and recommends honest, direct communication.

### Governing Influences

- **Ògúndá Méjì** — struggle, tools, perseverance, clearing the path (Ògún)
- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)


### Guidance

Settle workplace tensions quickly and fairly.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ògúndá Méjì (single · single · single · double) and the left column is Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (double · double · double · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ògúndá-Ọ̀kànràn means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ògúndá Méjì

Ògúndá honours the warrior spirit of Ògún — perseverance, the courage to clear obstacles, and the discipline of using one's tools and skills wisely rather than recklessly.

*Ọ̀nà tí a kò gbóná, a kì í rìn án láìsí ọgbọ́n.*  
"A path that has not been cleared cannot be walked without skill and effort."

### From Ọ̀kànràn Méjì

Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. It teaches that confronting conflict honestly, though painful, prevents greater harm later.

*Òtítọ́ máa ń dùn, ṣùgbọ́n irọ́ máa ń pa ni níkẹyìn.*  
"Truth may sting, but a lie eventually destroys."

### Putting the two together

This figure reads as encouragement to keep working at a difficult task and to prepare the right tools before starting. It also cautions that power without restraint becomes destructive, so strength should be directed by purpose. At the same time, in everyday terms, this figure advises resolving disputes directly rather than letting them grow in silence. It also asks a person to think before speaking, because words once said cannot be taken back.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
