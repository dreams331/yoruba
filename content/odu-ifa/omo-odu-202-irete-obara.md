## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀bàrà

### Meaning

Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀bàrà combines the energy of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory) with Ọ̀bàrà Méjì (honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)

### Guidance

When Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀bàrà appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀bàrà Méjì — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership. Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì advises: Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. Ọ̀bàrà Méjì adds: Ọ̀bàrà teaches that leadership and confidence are gifts, but boastfulness invites downfall. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀bàrà is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
