## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Ọ̀bàrà

### Overview

Endurance without arrogance. Success after a long struggle can make a person proud, and pride can undo it.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)


### Guidance

Stay humble when the win finally comes.
