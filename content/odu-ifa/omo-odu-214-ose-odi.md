## Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òdí

### Meaning

Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òdí combines the energy of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude) with Òdí Méjì (the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)
- **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òdí appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude — must be balanced with the lessons of Òdí Méjì — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì advises: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Òdí Méjì adds: Òdí teaches patience with processes that are not yet visible — pregnancy, growth, and transformation take time in darkness before they emerge into light. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Òdí is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
