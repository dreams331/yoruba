## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Òdí

### Overview

An ending is quietly preparing a rebirth. Like a seed in the earth, something that seems finished is transforming out of sight.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)


### Guidance

Do not mourn too long. Prepare for what is growing beneath the surface.

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Òdí is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
