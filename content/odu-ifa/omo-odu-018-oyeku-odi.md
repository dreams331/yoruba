## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Òdí

### Meaning

Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Òdí combines the energy of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality) with Òdí Méjì (the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Òdí appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality — must be balanced with the lessons of Òdí Méjì — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì advises: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Òdí Méjì adds: Òdí teaches patience with processes that are not yet visible — pregnancy, growth, and transformation take time in darkness before they emerge into light. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Òdí is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
