## Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ìrẹtẹ̀

### Meaning

Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ìrẹtẹ̀ combines the energy of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude) with Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)
- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ìrẹtẹ̀ appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude — must be balanced with the lessons of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì advises: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì adds: Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ìrẹtẹ̀ is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
