## Òtúrá-Ọ̀ṣẹ́

### Meaning

Òtúrá-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ combines the energy of Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed) with Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)
- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)

### Guidance

When Òtúrá-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude. Òtúrá Méjì advises: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì adds: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òtúrá-Ọ̀ṣẹ́ is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
