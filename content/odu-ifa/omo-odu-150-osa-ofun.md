## Ọ̀sá-Òfún

### Overview

After the whirlwind, a clean slate. It speaks of starting afresh with a clear conscience and renewed purity of purpose.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)


### Guidance

Use the reset to begin again, with integrity.

---

*Ọ̀sá-Òfún is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
