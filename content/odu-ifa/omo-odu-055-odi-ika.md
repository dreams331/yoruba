## Òdí-Ìká

### Overview

Hidden malice needs careful handling. The figure urges patience and discretion in dealing with someone whose intentions are unclear.

### Governing Influences

- **Òdí Méjì** — the womb, mystery, transformation, hidden depth (Ọya / Yemọja)
- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)


### Guidance

Be discreet, avoid confrontation, and rely on prudence rather than suspicion.

---

*Òdí-Ìká is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
