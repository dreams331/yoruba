## Ọ̀kànràn-Ìwòrì

### Meaning

Ọ̀kànràn-Ìwòrì combines the energy of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences) with Ìwòrì Méjì (self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀kànràn-Ìwòrì appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences — must be balanced with the lessons of Ìwòrì Méjì — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision. Ọ̀kànràn Méjì advises: Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. Ìwòrì Méjì adds: Ìwòrì calls for introspection and honesty about one's own motives before judging others. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀kànràn-Ìwòrì is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
