## Ọ̀kànràn-Ìwòrì

### Overview

Honest speech joins self-awareness. The figure says that you can say hard things well only after you have looked honestly at yourself.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Check your own part in a quarrel before you speak.
