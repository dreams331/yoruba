## Ọ̀sá-Ìrosùn

### Overview

A storm demands sacrifice. The figure advises making an offering or a sacrifice of comfort so that the damage stays small.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)
- **Ìrosùn Méjì** — blood, sacrifice, warning, consequence (Ṣàngó / Ọya)


### Guidance

Give up something small now to avoid a larger loss.

---

*Ọ̀sá-Ìrosùn is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
