## Òfún-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Meaning

Òfún-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú combines the energy of Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity) with Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)

### Guidance

When Òfún-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality. Òfún Méjì advises: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì adds: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òfún-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
