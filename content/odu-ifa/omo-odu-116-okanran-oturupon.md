## Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrúpọ̀n

### Meaning

Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrúpọ̀n combines the energy of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences) with Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)
- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrúpọ̀n appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences — must be balanced with the lessons of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience. Ọ̀kànràn Méjì advises: Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì adds: Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀kànràn-Òtúrúpọ̀n is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
