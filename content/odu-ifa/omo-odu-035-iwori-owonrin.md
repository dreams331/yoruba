## Ìwòrì-Ọ̀wọ́nrín

### Overview

Insight meets instability. Changing circumstances demand that you see clearly, adjust quickly and remain humble.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì** — instability, change, the trickster's lesson, humility (Èṣù / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)


### Guidance

Stay observant and flexible, and do not cling to plans that no longer fit.

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Ìwòrì Méjì (double · single · single · double) and the left column is Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì (double · double · single · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ìwòrì-Ọ̀wọ́nrín means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Ìwòrì Méjì

Ìwòrì calls for introspection and honesty about one's own motives before judging others. It reveals secrets — both dangerous and beneficial — and teaches that true wisdom begins with self-awareness.

*Bí a kò bá mọ ara ẹni, a kì í mọ ẹlòmíràn.*  
"If one does not know oneself, one cannot truly know another."

### From Ọ̀wọ́nrín Méjì

Ọ̀wọ́nrín teaches humility in success and resilience in reversal. It reminds us that fortunes change, so one must remain adaptable, avoid arrogance, and treat others well on the way up.

*Ohun tó dúró jẹ́ẹ́, ni yíò yí padà.*  
"What stands firm today may be overturned tomorrow."

### Putting the two together

Tradition uses this figure to counsel reflection. Before blaming, ask what part is yours. Before trusting a story, look for what is missing from it. Insight gained this way can protect a person from deception, including self-deception. At the same time, this figure suggests that a position held today can change tomorrow, so kindness and good relationships are better security than status. It also advises preparing for uncertainty rather than pretending it will not come.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
