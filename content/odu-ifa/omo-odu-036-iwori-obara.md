## Ìwòrì-Ọ̀bàrà

### Overview

Knowing oneself checks arrogance. The figure says that honest self-assessment protects against the pitfalls of pride and showing off.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìwòrì Méjì** — self-knowledge, hidden truth, inner vision (Ọ̀sanyìn / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ọ̀bàrà Méjì** — honour, boastfulness, the danger of pride, leadership (Ṣàngó / Ọ̀bàtálá)


### Guidance

Measure yourself honestly and let your character, not your boasting, earn respect.
