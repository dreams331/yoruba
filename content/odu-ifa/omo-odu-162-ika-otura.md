## Ìká-Òtúrá

### Meaning

Ìká-Òtúrá combines the energy of Ìká Méjì (cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint) with Òtúrá Méjì (transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)

### Guidance

When Ìká-Òtúrá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìká Méjì — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint — must be balanced with the lessons of Òtúrá Méjì — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed. Ìká Méjì advises: Ìká warns of the dangers of bitterness, envy, and vengeance — both suffering it and inflicting it. Òtúrá Méjì adds: Òtúrá teaches that confusion and difficulty are often temporary, giving way to clarity for those who remain patient and truthful. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìká-Òtúrá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
