## Ìká-Òtúrá

### Overview

Clarity dissolves anger. Once the facts are clear, resentment loses its force.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)
- **Òtúrá Méjì** — transformation, clarity after confusion, truth revealed (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọbàtálá)


### Guidance

Seek the whole story before judging.

---

*Ìká-Òtúrá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
