## Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀kànràn

### Meaning

Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀kànràn combines the energy of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality) with Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀kànràn appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì advises: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Ọ̀kànràn Méjì adds: Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀kànràn is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
