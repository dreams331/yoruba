## Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú

### Meaning

Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú combines the energy of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì (illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience) with Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì (endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì** — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience (Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà)
- **Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì** — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)

### Guidance

When Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì — illness and healing, secrets of the body, patience — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality. Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì advises: Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì adds: Ọ̀yẹ̀kú teaches respect for the ancestors, acceptance of mortality, and the understanding that every ending clears space for renewal. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Òtúrúpọ̀n-Ọ̀yẹ̀kú is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
