## Ogbè-Ìrẹtẹ̀

### Overview

Success is delayed but not denied. A setback is only a stage; the figure confirms that persistence brings a hidden victory.

### Governing Influences

* **Èjì Ogbè** — light, new beginnings, divine potential (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Olódùmarè)
* **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)


### Guidance

Keep going through disappointment. Your effort is being rewarded in ways not yet visible.

<!-- READING_START -->

### How this figure is read

In Ifá, a figure is built from two columns of four marks, read from the right column to the left. Here the right column is Èjì Ogbè (single · single · single · single) and the left column is Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (single · single · double · single). Many diviners start with the right column and then bring in the left, though schools of Ifá differ in how they weigh the two. Reading Ogbè-Ìrẹtẹ̀ means holding both lessons together and asking how they bear on the question at hand.

### From Èjì Ogbè

You were not born by accident. Every person carries an ori (destiny) chosen before birth. Ogbè teaches that your task is to remember and fulfil your purpose, and to lead with clarity, honesty and generosity of spirit.

*Imọ́lẹ̀ kìí ṣe n̄ǹkan tí a fún ọ — imọ́lẹ̀ ni ọ jẹ́.*  
"Light is not something given to you — light is what you are."

### From Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì

Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. It reassures that persistence — even quiet, unglamorous effort — leads to eventual triumph.

*Ìṣẹ́gun tí a kò fojú rí ni í dùn jù.*  
"The victory unseen by others is often the sweetest."

### Putting the two together

When this figure is cast, tradition reads it as encouragement to act openly. Plan in the light, speak plainly, and avoid shortcuts that need to be hidden later. It also reminds a person that a good start still needs steady follow-through. At the same time, this figure reads as reassurance for those who feel stuck. Keep going, keep records of progress, and avoid measuring yourself against people whose circumstances differ. Results often arrive later than hoped but are lasting when earned.

Interpretations of individual Omo Odù differ between lineages and between diviners, so treat this page as a reading aid and not a ruling. A consultation with a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá will always be fuller than any written summary.
