## Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì

> *"Àrùn tí a kò tọ́jú lọ́nà tí ó tọ́ kì í yá."*
> *"An illness not treated in the right way does not heal."*

---

### Meaning

The Odu of illness and healing — patience with the body and the wisdom of proper treatment.

### Associated Òrìṣà

Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà

### Life Lesson

Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. It also symbolises the wisdom that comes from surviving hardship.

### Overview

Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì is associated with illness, recovery and the body. In the tradition's reading, health is something to understand carefully, not to rush. The figure also stands for the strength of those who have come through hardship.

### Core Themes

- Proper diagnosis before action
- Patience in healing
- Respect for the body and its limits
- Wisdom gained from surviving difficulty

### Everyday Guidance

Read in daily life, this figure advises seeking the right help early, following treatment through, and resting enough. It also encourages care for people who are unwell, since recovery is helped by support.

### Reflection

Am I paying attention to what my body and mind are telling me?

### In the Wider System

This is a principal figure (Odù Méjì). It is the first parent of 15 of the 240 compound figures (Omo Odù), and the second parent of another 15. Each of those inherits something of this figure's character and adds the influence of its partner.
