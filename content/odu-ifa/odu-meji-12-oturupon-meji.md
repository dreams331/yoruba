## Òtúrúpọ̀n Méjì

> *"Àrùn tí a kò tọ́jú lọ́nà tí ó tọ́ kì í yá."*
> *"An illness not treated in the right way does not heal."*

---

### Meaning

The Odu of illness and healing — patience with the body and the wisdom of proper treatment.

### Associated Òrìṣà

Ọbàtálá / Ọ̀rúnmìlà

### Life Lesson

Òtúrúpọ̀n calls for patience, proper diagnosis before action, and respect for the body's natural healing processes. It also symbolises the wisdom that comes from surviving hardship.

---

*This is one of the 16 principal Odù Ifá (Odù Méjì) — the senior figures from which all 240 Omo Odù are formed by combination.*
