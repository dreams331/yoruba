## Ìká-Ọ̀kànràn

### Meaning

Ìká-Ọ̀kànràn combines the energy of Ìká Méjì (cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint) with Ọ̀kànràn Méjì (conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìká Méjì** — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint (Ọya / Èṣù)
- **Ọ̀kànràn Méjì** — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences (Ọya / Ògún)

### Guidance

When Ìká-Ọ̀kànràn appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìká Méjì — cruelty, resentment, the need for restraint — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀kànràn Méjì — conflict, truth-telling, sharp consequences. Ìká Méjì advises: Ìká warns of the dangers of bitterness, envy, and vengeance — both suffering it and inflicting it. Ọ̀kànràn Méjì adds: Ọ̀kànràn exposes what is hidden and warns against betrayal, gossip, and dishonesty. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìká-Ọ̀kànràn is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
