## Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òfún

### Meaning

Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òfún combines the energy of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì (perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory) with Òfún Méjì (purity, old age, completion, moral integrity). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì** — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory (Ọ̀rúnmìlà / Ọya)
- **Òfún Méjì** — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity (Ọbàtálá / Olódùmarè)

### Guidance

When Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òfún appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì — perseverance despite setbacks, hidden victory — must be balanced with the lessons of Òfún Méjì — purity, old age, completion, moral integrity. Ìrẹtẹ̀ Méjì advises: Ìrẹtẹ̀ teaches resilience in adversity, especially struggles others cannot see. Òfún Méjì adds: Òfún, closely tied to Ọbàtálá, teaches purity of character, patience, and the moral discipline required to age gracefully and finish life's journey with integrity intact. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ìrẹtẹ̀-Òfún is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
