## Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ọ̀sá

### Meaning

Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ọ̀sá combines the energy of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì (blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude) with Ọ̀sá Méjì (upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm). It is read as a distinct Odù with its own guidance, drawing on both influences.

### Governing Influences

- **Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì** — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude (Ọ̀ṣun)
- **Ọ̀sá Méjì** — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)

### Guidance

When Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ọ̀sá appears, it suggests a situation where the lessons of Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì — blessings, sweetness, abundance, gratitude — must be balanced with the lessons of Ọ̀sá Méjì — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm. Ọ̀ṣẹ́ Méjì advises: Ọ̀ṣẹ́ celebrates the sweetness of life earned through generosity, hospitality, and gratitude. Ọ̀sá Méjì adds: Ọ̀sá teaches resilience through sudden disruption. Together, they call for wisdom that honours both sides of this Odù.

---

*Ọ̀ṣẹ́-Ọ̀sá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.*
