About Yoruba Heritage

Preserving and sharing authentic Yoruba history, culture, and wisdom

Our Mission

Yoruba Heritage is dedicated to preserving, documenting, and sharing the rich cultural legacy of the Yoruba people. We believe that understanding our past is essential to shaping a meaningful future.

Our platform serves as a comprehensive digital archive of Yoruba history, traditional wisdom, cultural practices, and contemporary developments. We are committed to providing factual, verified information that honors the complexity and depth of Yoruba civilization.

Through careful research and collaboration with scholars, elders, and community members worldwide, we aim to create a resource that educates, inspires, and connects Yoruba people and anyone interested in this remarkable culture.

Our Core Values

Authenticity

We prioritize accurate, verified information sourced from scholarly research and authentic oral traditions.

Respect

We honor the wisdom of our ancestors and the living traditions of Yoruba communities worldwide.

Community

We believe in building connections among Yoruba people and enthusiasts across the globe.

Education

We are committed to making knowledge accessible and engaging for all generations.

Editorial Standards

Yoruba Heritage is an independent educational project. Our aim is to explain Yoruba history, language, belief and daily life accurately and in plain English, for readers in Nigeria and across the diaspora.

How we write

Articles and stories are written and edited by our editorial team in our own words. We draw on published histories, academic work, museum and heritage-body material. Where a page relies on a particular source, we link to it. We do not copy text from other websites.

Religion and tradition

Ifá, the Òrìṣà and related practices vary between towns, lineages and diviners. Our Odù Ifá pages are reading aids for education and cultural understanding. They are not a substitute for consulting a trained Babaláwo or Ìyánífá, and they should not be used for medical, legal or financial decisions.

News and events

Ìròyìn summarises reports from named publishers and links to the original article. Event dates change from year to year, so please check with the organisers before travelling.

Corrections

If you find an error, or you know a local tradition that differs from what we have written, please tell us through the contact page. We welcome input from elders, scholars and community members.
