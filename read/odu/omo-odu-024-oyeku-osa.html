Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀sá

Overview

Turbulence stirs the realm of the ancestors. The figure warns of sudden upheaval and advises calm, protective action.

Governing Influences

  • Ọ̀yẹ̀kú Méjì — endings, the ancestors, the unseen world, mortality (Ikú (Death) / Ọ̀sun)
  • Ọ̀sá Méjì — upheaval, the whirlwind, sudden change, protection from harm (Ọya)

Guidance

Stay composed in a crisis and seek spiritual cover before making big decisions.

---

Ọ̀yẹ̀kú-Ọ̀sá is one of the 240 Omo Odù formed by combining two of the 16 principal Odù Méjì. Naming conventions for Omo Odù can vary between Ifá lineages; this entry uses a systematic right–left naming for clarity.
